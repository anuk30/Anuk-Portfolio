ROBOTICS · AUTOMATION

Smart Wall Painting Robot

A three-mode autonomous painting robot controlled through a custom-built mobile app over Bluetooth (HC-06). The robot runs on an Arduino Mega, with four geared motors driven by two L298N drivers for Mecanum-wheel locomotion, and a NEMA 17 stepper (via A4988 driver) for precision axis movement.

  • Wall Painting: Uses PID control to automatically align itself with the wall, then paints layer by layer for even coverage.
  • Letter Painting: Traces specific letters sent from the app using a hardcoded matrix-based coordinate system.
  • Shape Painting: Draws selected shapes using the matrix-based approach.

Paint is applied through a diaphragm pump and spray mechanism, actuated via a relay module.
